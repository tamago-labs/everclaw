Everclaw Enables Private Local AI + Browser Automation with kane-cli and automates it on a schedule. Built from scratch with Muse Spark 1.2 with OpenCode by installing a kane-cli skill for the product test loop during development and leveraging it as a claw at runtime for scheduled browser work — price checks, health checks, product assertions on `amazon.com/dp/B08N5WRWNW`, or daily guide scraping. Runs entirely on-device via QVAC with four builtin models (Qwen 1.7B/4B, Gemma 4B/31B) plus custom HuggingFace imports; private by design, sensitive data and credentials in `~/.everclaw` and Variables never leave your machine. Inspired by OpenClaw, it keeps multi-session persistence with reliable long-running Cron execution, and is open-source.
